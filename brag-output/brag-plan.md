# Brag Plan: LexFlow

## What is this app?
LexFlow turns Microsoft 365 shared-mailbox conversations into owned, SLA-visible tasks with automated routing, escalation, vacation coverage, and an auditable completion record.

## The angle
Shared inboxes are not the problem; invisible ownership is. The film follows one urgent message as LexFlow routes it, protects its SLA, covers an away teammate, and closes the loop.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
An urgent email lands with the line: **“An inbox is not an owner.”** The message snaps into a named workflow.

## Key moments
- A real LexFlow-style inbox conversation is routed to Legal with a visible owner and SLA.
- Vacation Mode reroutes intended work to the next available teammate without losing the original rule intent.
- The task completes with an audit trail and the queue returns to a controlled state.

## Outro / punchline
**Every message has an owner. Every handoff has a record.**

## User flow worth showing
Email arrives → automation rule assigns an accountable owner → SLA and vacation coverage keep it moving → completion is recorded.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium operations command center
- Interpretation: dark, restrained, decisive motion; product proof first; no generic SaaS claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: #FBFBFA
- Accent: #C94B31
- Text: #11110F
- Display font: Avenir Next / Helvetica Neue
- Body font: Avenir Next / Helvetica Neue
- Strongest visual element: conversation rows moving through rule, owner, SLA, vacation coverage, and completion states.

## Share copy (draft)
Shared inboxes create work. LexFlow creates ownership—routing every conversation, protecting every SLA, and recording every handoff.

## Audio direction
- Role: restrained rhythmic support
- Music: Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 12
- Music treatment: low bed, short fade-in, clean final fade
- Music cue guidance: major landings at the route assignment, vacation handoff, and final lockup
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle glow and status pulses only
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched interface accents
- Audio-coupled moments: incoming email, rule match, handoff, completion
- Restraint rule: never score every UI movement

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The problem — 3.2s
An urgent message arrives against the LexFlow dark canvas. “An inbox is not an owner.”
Sequential/interaction: message card lands, then the headline resolves.
Audio intent: one controlled impact.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Ownership — 4.3s
A LexFlow conversation task routes through a priority rule to Maya Shah in Legal. SLA begins at 01:59:42.
Sequential/interaction: Inbox → Priority rule → Owner accountable.
Audio intent: precise interface ticks.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Coverage — 4.2s
Vacation Mode is active for Maya. LexFlow preserves rule intent and moves the task to Jordan, with a return-briefing record.
Sequential/interaction: away toggle activates, task glides to the next available teammate.
Audio intent: restrained swoosh and confirmation tone.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outcome — 4.3s
Jordan completes the conversation. SLA turns healthy, the audit trail records the action, and the queue count drops.
Sequential/interaction: completion check, audit event, KPI count.
Audio intent: one positive completion bell.
Transition mood: slow fade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Brand close — 4s
LexFlow lockup with “Every message has an owner. Every handoff has a record.”
Sequential/interaction: logo, line, Microsoft 365 descriptor.
Audio intent: music resolves and fades.
